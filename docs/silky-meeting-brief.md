# Silky — Meeting Brief

Prep for showing Silky her site, agreeing on go-live, and setting expectations.
Keep it **non-technical** and **hands-on** — let her touch it.

---

## ⭐ Before the meeting (do this first)
**Deploy to a free `something.pages.dev` URL** (no domain needed yet) so Silky can open it
**on her own phone** during the meeting. Live-on-her-phone is 10× the wow of a laptop demo,
and it's free + reversible. Best prep move there is.

Also have ready:
- The admin password
- 1–2 of *her real* pieces loaded (so the demo feels like **hers**, not placeholder)

---

## 1. What to SHOW her — demo in this order (build the wow)
1. **The public site** — open her homepage on the phone. Collage design, a story, night mode, the radio. *"This is your site. It's real."*
2. **Let HER write** — `/admin` → "new piece" → hand her the device. She types, pastes from notes, drops a photo → **Publish** → refresh live site, it's there. *The moment she gets it.*
3. **Her homepage, her way** — *your homepage → add section*: a **venting** entry (mood doodle), a **photo gallery**, reorder them.
4. **Readers talk back** — a like + comment on a post, then the **moderation** screen where she deletes one.
5. **Share** — share button → sending a post to WhatsApp/Instagram.
6. Mention: **preview before publishing**, drafts, built to **rank on Google**.

~15 min. Let her drive.

---

## 2. What to TELL her (talking points)
- *"A **custom site + your own control panel** — not WordPress, not a template. Everything is on **your** site; you never log into anyone else's."*
- *"You publish yourself, like Google Docs. No coding, ever. I handle all the technical stuff."*
- *"It's your **digital diary** — you decide the sections: stories, venting, books, music, photos."*
- **Anchor the value:** *"This is a ₹50,000–1,00,000 build. You're my first client, so it's free — I'll use your site to get paying ones."*
- **Her role:** *"You're the writer. Content + sections are yours. Design + tech are mine."*

---

## 3. What we've DONE (in her language)
- Her **own website** with a bespoke handmade design (not a template)
- A **Google-Docs-style editor** — write, paste, upload photos (auto-optimized so pages stay fast)
- A **homepage she composes** from sections (stories, venting-with-moods, books, music, photo galleries)
- **Comments + likes** so readers interact — and **she moderates**
- **Share** buttons, **SEO** to rank, **mobile-friendly**, night mode, the radio
- **Preview before publishing**, drafts, one-of-each sections so it never gets messy

---

## 4. How she'll USE it (the whole workflow)
1. Go to **yoursite.com/admin**, log in
2. **Write** → Preview → **Publish**
3. **Your homepage** → add / edit / reorder sections
4. **Comments** tab → delete anything unwanted

> That's the entire job. Everything else is handled.

---

## 5. Decisions to GET FROM her (checklist)
- [ ] **Name** — is "moonlit" final, or something else?
- [ ] **Domain** — what web address? (e.g. `silkywrites.com`)
- [ ] **Launch sections** — which does she want on day one?
- [ ] **Starter content** — 3–5 pieces + an "About" blurb + a photo of her. *Who writes what, by when?*
- [ ] **Password** — she picks it
- [ ] **Scope agreement** — *"Here's what's included; big new features later are a separate conversation."*

---

## 6. How much time to LIVE
- **Technically:** basically **done** — deployable in **1–2 days** once the domain is bought and I deploy.
- **Realistically:** the variable is **her content**. Target: *"Get me ~5 pieces + your About by [DATE], we go live the week of [DATE]."*
- **Path:** **soft launch** on the free `.pages.dev` URL (she plays a few days) → buy domain → **public launch**.

---

## 7. What needs MAINTENANCE (who does what)
Frame: *"You just write. I handle the tech. Once a year we renew the domain."*

| Thing | Who | How often | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Writing / content | **Silky** | ongoing | zero tech |
| Comment moderation | **Silky** | as needed | delete spam in admin |
| Hosting | Cloudflare | — | **free**, nothing to do |
| Domain renewal | **You** | ~₹900/yr | handle it for her |
| Bug fixes / updates / features | **You** | as needed | light care plan; **first year free** |
| **Backups of her writing** | **You** | monthly | ⚠️ export the D1 database so content is never lost |
| Security (passwords/tech) | **You** | — | you own it |

**Do not skip backups** — her words live in Cloudflare's database; a monthly export means a
mistake or outage never loses her diary. (Ask Claude to build a one-command backup.)

---

## 8. Meeting flow (30–40 min)
1. **Show** on her phone (10) → **let her publish** (10) → **sections + comments + share** (10)
2. **Offer + value + her role** (5)
3. **Decisions + timeline + who-does-what** (5–10)
4. End with a **date** + a **content homework list** for her.

## 9. Pitfalls to avoid
- **Don't get technical** — keep it about *her* experience.
- **Don't over-promise in the room.** New feature request → *"Love it — let's launch first, then look at that."*
- **Don't leave without a go-live date + her content deadline.**
